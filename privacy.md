Short version: this is a static site. It has no accounts, no comments, no newsletter form and no advertising.

## What this site collects

**Nothing directly.** There is no form on this site, no login, no comment system and no tracking script placed by CoinConnect.

## What third parties see

- **Our hosting provider** processes your IP address and browser user agent in order to serve pages to you, as any web host does. It is used to deliver the site and for security, not to identify you.
- **Google Fonts** serves the typeface used here. Requesting a font sends your IP address to Google's servers.

Neither is used by CoinConnect to identify or profile you.

## Cookies

This site sets no cookies of its own.

## Analytics

No analytics are installed at the time of writing. If that changes, this page is updated before the change goes live, and any analytics used will be configured to collect aggregate traffic data only.

## The main CoinConnect site

[coinconnect.site]({{ site.parent.url }}) is a separate system with its own contact forms and its own privacy handling. This page covers only the blog subdomain.

## Contact

For any question about data on this site, contact CoinConnect through the [main site]({{ site.parent.url }}).
